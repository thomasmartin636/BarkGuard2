Privacy

Last updated September 2026

Microphone audio stays on your device

Bark Guard analyzes sound in your browser in real time. Audio is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. Your settings, bark log and daily usage are saved only in your browser's local storage.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. Stripe shares your email address and subscription status with us so we can unlock Unlimited and help with billing questions. See Stripe's privacy policy.

Email

If you ask to restore a subscription on a new device, we email you a sign-in link. We don't send marketing email.
