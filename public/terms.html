Last updated September 2026

What Bark Guard is

Bark Guard is a web app that listens through your device's microphone for barking and plays a high-frequency tone. It is a training aid, not a guarantee: results vary by dog, device and room. Stop using it if your dog seems frightened or distressed.

Free plan

You can use Bark Guard for up to one hour of listening per day for free. The hour resets at midnight on your device.

Unlimited subscription

Unlimited removes the daily limit. It costs $2.99 per month or $19.99 per year (plus any tax), billed in advance through Stripe, and renews automatically until you cancel. You can cancel any time from Manage subscription in the app; you keep unlimited access until the end of the period you've paid for.

Refunds

If Bark Guard isn't working for you, email support@nobarks.com within 14 days of a charge and we'll refund it in full.
